## 3. What the app is for

Rugby Timer helps match officials keep time, track sin-bins and cards, and record the score. It's a tool to support the referee, not a replacement for one:

- **You stay in charge.** Decisions on the field, and the official record of the match, remain the responsibility of the match officials and the competition.
- **Check your competition's rules.** The app offers common settings (half length, sin-bin length, 20-minute red cards, extra time and more), but laws and competition regulations vary and change. Make sure the settings you use match the match you're refereeing.
- **Have a backup.** Devices can run out of battery, be damaged or behave unexpectedly. For important matches, keep a backup way of timing the match.
