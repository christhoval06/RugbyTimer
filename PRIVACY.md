# Privacy Policy

**Rugby Timer** for iPhone and Apple Watch, and this website\
Effective date: November 12, 2026

Rugby Timer is a match clock for rugby referees. It's built so that your matches stay on your devices. This policy explains what the app handles and where that data goes.

## The short version

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
- The app doesn't send your data to any server, including ours.
- Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

## Data the app handles

| Data | What it's for | Where it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team names, half length, sin-bin length, half-time break, shirt-number range | Setting up a match | On your iPhone, and sent to your paired Apple Watch |
| Scores, cards, shirt numbers and the match timeline | Running the match and building the match record | On the device running the match clock (your Apple Watch, or your iPhone in iPhone-only mode), saved in the app's own storage so the match survives the app closing. It's replaced by the next match and isn't saved to a server. |
| Finished matches: teams, final score and the match timeline | The match history on your iPhone | On your iPhone, in the app's own storage, until you delete the match from the history. A Quick Match run on the Watch alone is sent to your paired iPhone at full time for its history. Never saved to a server. |
| Preferences (default teams, haptics, keep screen awake, tap lock, coin toss, language) | Remembering your settings | On your device, in the app's local settings |
| Team names, score, match clock and sin-bins | The Live Activity on your iPhone Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, the Match widget, and the Watch complication | Shared on the same device between the app and its widgets. A Live Activity and Lock Screen widgets can be seen without unlocking the phone; turn the Live Activity off in Settings, or remove the widget, if you don't want that. |
| Apple Watch model name and battery level | Showing the Watch status on the iPhone Setup screen | Sent from your Watch to your paired iPhone only |

Team names and shirt numbers are whatever you type in. The app doesn't ask for players' names or any other personal details.

## iPhone and Apple Watch sync

The iPhone and Watch apps talk to each other through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. That connection runs directly between your paired devices, over Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi. Match data isn't sent to us or to anyone else along the way.

## Permissions

- **Notifications (Apple Watch):** used for local alerts at half-time, at the end of the half-time break and when a sin-bin ends. These alerts are scheduled on the Watch itself. The app doesn't use push notifications from a server.
- **Health (Apple Watch, optional):** if you allow it, the Watch runs each match as a Rugby workout. That keeps the clock and alerts running for the whole match, even with your wrist down, and saves the workout to the Health app at full time. While the workout runs, the Watch reads your heart rate, distance and active energy for that workout. This data goes only to Apple Health on your devices. Rugby Timer doesn't send it anywhere or use it for anything else. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Health on your iPhone.
- **Background running (Apple Watch):** if you don't allow Health access, the Watch app uses an extended runtime session during each half and the half-time break instead. That doesn't read any health data.

The app doesn't request your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## Sharing a match record

When a match ends, you can share the match record as text. It goes through the standard iOS share sheet, to the app or person you pick. The app keeps the match in your history on the iPhone, but doesn't send it anywhere else. Once shared, the receiving app's privacy policy applies.

## Contacting support and external links

If you email support from Settings, your email app opens with the app version filled in. We only see what you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you.

Settings also links to the developer's website and GitHub. They open in your browser and are covered by their own privacy policies.

## This website

The Rugby Timer website doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it loads its fonts from its own server rather than a third party. If you pick a language on the site, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it; you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data. Like any website, the server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure. The App Store button takes you to Apple's App Store, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

Rugby Timer is meant for match officials. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

## Deleting your data

Swipe a match in **Match History** to delete it. Use **Restore default settings** in Settings to reset your preferences. The language setting is kept. To remove everything the app has stored, delete Rugby Timer from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
